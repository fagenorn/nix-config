# ADR-claims-013 — A merge needs the land lock at its head and a fresh lease

On 26 Nov a chain of takeovers, not the lock, decided which pull request merged: only the landing step's own recheck kept a lander from merging, and a takeover between that recheck and the merge went unseen. The lifecycle guard and the console's merge permit now refuse a command-line merge unless the land lock of its branch (the base, or for a release pull request its head branch, ADR-claims-009) is at the merge's head and still there after the other reads, and the merge pins that head with `--match-head-commit`; when that pull request has a lease, its newest lease is at that head with a beat younger than `LEASE_LAPSE` minus `MERGE_MARGIN`, and leases all dated ahead of the forge's clock refuse. A holder with no lease of its own, a direct landing or a release, is judged by the lock alone, and a repository with no land lock merges as before. The owner merges by hand only on the forge's page or the console's Merge card, so neither judges a hand merge. A batch train keeps its lock at its first member's head, so its car merge is refused until issue 1933 moves the lock to the car's head; trains are opt-in. Rejected: exempting a hand merge on the command line, which the forge cannot tell from a lander's, and leaving the check to the landing step. The cost is up to four forge reads per merge, and a failed read refuses the merge.
