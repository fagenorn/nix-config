# ADR-claims-005 — Only a waiting acquirer takes over an idle land lock, after 45 minutes

A dead holder leaves its lock at a head that never merges. On issue 629 the owner chose option B: a one-shot `status` or `acquire` takes over only the lock of a merged or closed pull request, and only `acquire --wait` takes over a held lock, once it has watched the lock unchanged for 45 minutes on its own monotonic clock, with nothing persisted. A holder whose lock stays at one sha through a long wait moves a second ref, the beat `refs/agent-locks/land-beat/<branch>`, and a waiter restarts its watch when the lock or the beat changes (issue 971). Rejected: judging idleness from event timestamps, which needs a wall clock and a persisted record; an unattended one-shot takeover, which could merge two branches at once; a holder releasing and retaking its lock to show it lives, which hands it to any waiter between. The cost is that a dead holder blocks one-shot acquirers until a waiter's 45 minutes pass, and that a holder keeping one sha for 45 minutes without refreshing loses its lock. A green lease (issue 1679) is also taken over by the first waiter in line when its beats stop for 3 minutes or it has held 40 (issue 1904, up from 20: a check took 30), by the forge's clock; a release and a batch train take no lease and keep the 45-minute takeover. A step with long commands starts `hold --detach`, a background beater that stops with its session. A late lease can cut a hold short.
