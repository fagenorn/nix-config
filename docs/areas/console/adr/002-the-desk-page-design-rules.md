# ADR-console-002 — The desk page's design rules

- **Status:** accepted
- **Decided in:** #833, #855, #887, #895, #911, #919

## Context

The owner chose the desk layout from a throwaway prototype that is never merged. Its rules are recorded here so later slices build the page without it.

## Decision

- **Structure.** A sticky top bar with the name, then a desk grid: at most 1360px wide, a main column `minmax(0, 1fr)` and a 460px side column with an 88px gap (400px and 56px under 1180px; one column under 900px; under 480px the top bar wraps and the statement shrinks). The statement leads the main column; the projects sit in the side column.
- **Statement.** One large line says whether the owner is needed: "Nothing needs you", or the number of inbox items in peach followed by "thing needs you" or "things need you". When the tracker could not be read and no item is known, it reads "The tracker cannot be read"; it never says "Nothing needs you" then. One plain sentence sits beneath it. Headline 650 weight, 3.5rem/1.04, display stack, -0.028em tracking (2.375rem, -0.022em under 900px); sentence 1.125rem/1.5 in Subtext 1, at most 44rem wide (1rem under 900px).
- **Inbox.** The inbox sits beneath the statement, ordered by held work. The first open item (the first waiting brief, else the first tracker item) is the one raised card: Surface 0, 16px radius, a head line with its kind in words and its icon (Decision, Brief, Check, Approval, Conflict, Label), its project and, at the right, how long it has waited. Its question is the heading (a brief's heading stays "<project> brief, version N"), with "and N more questions" when there are more; a decision shows a question label above its options only when the item has more than one question; below the options one line says what it holds ("holds n issues", with "read" and the time of the last good read when its tracker read is stale), and the page shows no issue number. Each option is a full-width button: its number as a key badge, its words, and "Recommended" on the recommended one; the picked option carries `aria-pressed="true"`, a Lavender border and a filled badge, so it is never marked by colour alone. The card ends in exactly one primary action, peach with Crust text: "Send answer" for a decision, "Approve" for a brief, the first answer for a check or an approval, and for an item the page cannot answer the "Answer on the tracker" link itself. The primary names its key only where a key acts on it (Enter, on the decision or brief the keys act on); a check's or an approval's primary names none. Beside it sit the quieter actions: the other answers, the note, the reply field and "Ask for changes", and a closed "Detail" fold holding the "Answer on the tracker" link, where a conflict or needs-label item also names its issue and pull request and an item's list shows. The undo, sending and "Could not send" words sit where the primary was. Every other open item is one row: icon and kind word, headline, project and held work, the wait at the right; its answer box, a pending Undo, a failure and its closed folds stay beneath that line. A quiet key strip under the inbox lists only the keys that act now: the number range and Enter for the decision the keys act on, Enter for a brief, Esc while an answer waits; it is absent otherwise. A waiting brief sits above every tracker item and, while it can be approved, takes the keys (ADR-console-013); otherwise the first answerable item (a decision with questions), wherever it sits in the list, is the one the keys act on, and a conflict or needs-label item leading the list never blocks it. A later waiting brief keeps its own unraised brief block, so its reply keeps focus. The options of that item are the buttons above, the tracker's chosen one (else the recommended one) preselected; a number key selects an option of the current question and moves to the next, Enter starts a five-second undo ("Sending in five seconds." with an "Undo" button; Escape undoes too), then the answer is sent. An item with a pending send keeps its answer box and its Undo wherever a frame moves it, and the keys then act on the next answerable item. Each answer shows one of four states: choosing, waiting, "Could not send: ..." in Red with the choice kept, or "Answered, waiting for the agent to pick it up". A held verification is an item of its own kind (ADR-console-012): its detail shows in full in a `pre` inside a closed fold ("Show the check", "Review and approve" for an approval, which also holds the confirmation line and field), and its buttons are by kind (a manual check "Passed" and "Failed", a verdict "Accept as passed" and "Close as not verified", an approval "Approve", a stale check "Keep waiting" and "Accept without the host"; an act prompt none), each behind the same undo. Besides a brief's reply, only two things are typed: a manual check's note in a textarea sent verbatim with "Send note", and the confirmation of every approval, which must equal `<owner>/<repo>#<issue>` before "Approve" enables (a paid one adds "This check costs money."). Number keys and Enter stay with a waiting brief and decision items and are ignored while an input, textarea, select or contenteditable element has focus; unsent text and focus survive every streamed frame. A decision prompt takes no typed reply (ADR-console-010), while a brief takes one. A brief is drawn as an open item: the goal above it, its version and its five parts, an empty list part shown as its label and "None" (#911), Enter starting the same five-second undo and then approving, and a reply field beneath that asks for changes (ADR-console-013). An answered item is one closed quiet Overlay 2 line, never peach: its question cut to one line with an ellipsis, then "answered <age>, waiting for the agent"; the whole item (its meta line, the choices and the link) shows when the line is opened. Every tracker item carries the "Answer on the tracker" link (in its Detail fold, or as the primary action when the page cannot answer it), the page's only link; a brief has none, because the page answers it itself.
- **Digest.** Beneath the inbox, "Since you last looked" with the time, or "In the last eight hours" on a first visit: five plain lines, newest first, each its age then its sentence, the rest under a closed "Earlier" fold with its count; "Nothing changed." when empty. Quiet colours only: Overlay 2 times, Subtext 0 sentences.
- **Times.** The page formats every time against its own clock in local time and never shows a raw timestamp. An age reads relative: "just now", "N min ago", "N h ago", "yesterday", "N days ago"; a duration ("waiting 3 h", "untouched for 3 days") reads "under a minute", "N min", "N h", "1 day", "N days"; a reset or a resume reads a weekday and 24-hour time ("Mon 05:00"). A delivery run's "moved 3 min ago" or "quiet for 22 min" is the same: its words come from the run's movement time. Each is a `time` element whose title is the exact local time ("2026-11-11 09:00"). The console sends only changed views, so the page moves its times itself every 30 seconds without a frame, touching nothing but their text.
- **Last looked.** The page tells the console it was looked at when a focus hold of more than five seconds ends; it sends nothing else but an answer (ADR-console-010).
- **Palette (Catppuccin Macchiato roles).** Base `#24273a` page; Mantle `#1e2030` sunken fields; Surface 0 `#363a4f` the one raised card; Surface 1 `#494d64` and Surface 2 `#5b6078` borders; Text `#cad3f5`; Subtext 1 `#b8c0e0` the sentence; Subtext 0 `#a5adcb` secondary text; Overlay 2 `#939ab7` quiet lines and times; Crust `#181926` the text on a filled peach or Lavender control; Lavender `#b7bdf8` the focus ring, the owner's own words and the picked option; Peach `#f5a97f` means "needs you" and nothing else, in three uses: the statement's count, an open item's kind word and the card's one primary action; Green `#a6da95`, Yellow `#eed49f` and Red `#ed8796` mark moving, waiting and stopped work; Red also marks "Could not send". Hairlines are `rgba(202, 211, 245, .09)`.
- **Type scale.** The system UI stack, never a web font. Body .9375rem/1.5 with tabular numerals; section headings 600 .9375rem/1.4; the lead question 600 1.5rem/1.3 display, the largest type after the statement headline; option words 1.0625rem and key badges .71875rem; secondary text .84375rem; small text .8125rem; quiet lines .875rem; the statement headline 2rem under 480px.
- **Projects.** A project is one line, a closed fold: its name in 600 Subtext 0, then its words in Overlay 2 ("quiet" with its icon and the untouched days), its sessions summary ("1 working, 1 waiting") and one sentence per delivery run of its sessions in Subtext 0. Its root, its sessions' names and states and each run's issue numbers and stages show only when the line is opened; with every line closed the page shows no path, session name or issue number. An unmanaged repository is the same line, named by the last segment of its root. Three or fewer stay lines; more than three fold into one closed group titled "N quiet projects". Projects with open work come first, then quiet ones, each by most recent activity. The view decides order and folding; the page only renders them.
- **Motion and small screens.** Motion stays under 200ms and is off under `prefers-reduced-motion`; under 900px the card's side padding drops to 16px.
- **State is never colour alone.** Every state carries a word, and every icon sits beside one.
- **Empty fleet.** With no registered project the projects section says "No project is registered yet."

## Consequences

- The prototype worktree can be dropped once this page ships.
- A new colour role or a second use of peach is a change to this record.
