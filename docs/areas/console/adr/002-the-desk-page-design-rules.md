# ADR-console-002 — The desk page's design rules

- **Status:** accepted; amended by ADR-console-024
- **Decided in:** #833, #855, #887, #895, #911, #919, #920, #922, #944, #1026

## Context

The owner chose the desk layout from a throwaway prototype that is never merged. Its rules are recorded here so later slices build the page without it.

## Decision

- **Structure.** A sticky top bar with the name, the goal field and the capacity strip, then a desk grid: at most 1360px wide, a main column `minmax(0, 1fr)` and a 460px side column with an 88px gap (400px and 56px under 1180px; one column under 900px; under 480px the top bar wraps and the statement shrinks). The statement leads the main column; the projects sit in the side column.
- **Statement.** One large line says whether the owner is needed: "Nothing needs you", or the number of inbox items in peach followed by "thing needs you" or "things need you". When the tracker could not be read and no item is known, it reads "The tracker cannot be read"; it never says "Nothing needs you" then. One plain sentence sits beneath it. Headline 650 weight, 3.5rem/1.04, display stack, -0.028em tracking (2.375rem, -0.022em under 900px); sentence 1.125rem/1.5 in Subtext 1, at most 44rem wide (1rem under 900px).
- **Goal field.** The goal field sits in the top bar: one line, "Tell a manager what you want next…", with its key `g` shown beside it. Focusing it or pressing `g` puts the cursor in it and opens the project choice (the filter, the recent projects, Send and Cancel) in a panel beneath it, at most 600px wide and never wider than the viewport less 32px; Escape or Cancel closes the panel, keeps the typed text and leaves the field. In focus the field shows one border, its container's Lavender one; the text area draws no ring of its own. Nothing under the statement opens it. Under 900px the field takes its own row of the top bar.
- **Capacity strip.** The strip starts with one state word beside its icon, fine, tight or out, by the view's tight and all-out rules: fine is a tick in Green, tight a clock in Yellow, out a stop in Red, never Peach. Then each profile with a reading: its name, cut at 9rem with an ellipsis and whole in its title, a 34px bar filled to its share of the week left whenever that share is known, out or not, and "N%", or "out until <weekday time>" when out, or "reset, not yet used" when every window has reset; the 5-hour share sits in its title. Then one time: "resets <weekday time>" for the earliest next reset of a profile that is not out, or "resumes <weekday time>" when every profile is out. Beside each listed profile sits its sign-in in Overlay 2 words ("signed in, ends in N days", "signed in, ends today", "signed in", "sign-in unknown"; a view from before sign-in adds none). A signed-out profile is one span, its name and "signed out", with no bar, percentage or out words, and its old reading never drives the state word or the reset time; a profile with no reading but a sign-in is one span of its name and its sign-in words, no bar. Both use Overlay 2, no new colour role. Profiles with no reading and no sign-in are one quiet note, and with no reading at all only the note shows. The all-out panel names a signed-out profile "<name> signed out", never out.
- **Inbox.** The inbox sits beneath the statement, ordered by held work. The first open item (the first waiting brief, else the first tracker item) is the one raised card: Surface 0, 16px radius, a head line with its kind in words and its icon (Decision, Brief, Check, Approval, Conflict, Label), its project and, at the right, how long it has waited. Its question is the heading (a brief's heading stays "<project> brief, version N"), with "and N more questions" when there are more; a decision shows a question label above its options only when the item has more than one question; below the options one line says what it holds ("holds n issues", with "read" and the time of the last good read when its tracker read is stale), and the page shows no issue number. Each option is a full-width button: its number as a key badge, its words, and "Recommended" on the recommended one; the picked option carries `aria-pressed="true"`, a Lavender border and a filled badge, so it is never marked by colour alone. The card ends in exactly one primary action, peach with Crust text: "Send answer" for a decision, "Approve" for a brief, the first answer for a check or an approval, and for an item the page cannot answer the "Answer on the tracker" link itself. The primary names its key only where a key acts on it (Enter, on the decision or brief the keys act on); a check's or an approval's primary names none. Beside it sit the quieter actions: the other answers, the note, the reply field and "Ask for changes", and a closed "Detail" fold holding the "Answer on the tracker" link, where a conflict or needs-label item also names its issue and pull request and an item's list shows. The undo, sending and "Could not send" words sit where the primary was. Every other open item is one row, a waiting brief included, set below the card by the same gap as between rows: icon and kind word, headline (a brief's goal), project and held work (a brief's project alone), the wait at the right; a tracker item's answer box, a pending Undo, a failure and its closed folds stay beneath that line. A quiet key strip under the inbox lists only the keys that act now: the number range for the decision the keys act on, Enter for it once every question has a pick, Enter for a brief unless its reply is sending, Esc while an answer waits; it is absent otherwise. A sign-in ask is a card of its own, never the raised card and never a tracker row: the headline, the sign-in words and the Sign in action, and while a sign-in runs its link, a field for the code and Cancel (ADR-console-024); sign-in cards sit after the stall and progress cards and before the questions and tracker items, in the order of the view's `inbox.open`. A waiting brief sits above every tracker item and, while it can be approved, takes the keys (ADR-console-013); otherwise the first answerable item (a decision with questions), wherever it sits in the list, is the one the keys act on, and a conflict or needs-label item leading the list never blocks it. A later waiting brief's row opens, by a click or Enter on it, to its whole unraised brief with its "Approve" and its reply field in place, and closing it keeps a typed reply; a row opens by itself once when its approval or reply gets under way, fails or is done, and the owner may close it again, and a row that receives the focused reply field when the items reorder opens so the field keeps its focus. When the card's item leaves, the next item becomes the card. The options of that item are the buttons above, the tracker's chosen one (else the recommended one) preselected; a number key selects an option of the current question and moves to the next, Enter starts a five-second undo ("Sending in five seconds." with an "Undo" button; Escape undoes too), then the answer is sent. An item with a pending send keeps its answer box and its Undo wherever a frame moves it, and the keys then act on the next answerable item. Each answer shows one of four states: choosing, waiting, "Could not send: ..." in Red with the choice kept, or "Answered, waiting for the agent to pick it up". A held verification is an item of its own kind (ADR-console-012): its detail shows in full in a `pre` inside a closed fold ("Show the check", "Review and approve" for an approval, which also holds the confirmation line and field), and its buttons are by kind (a manual check "Passed" and "Failed", a verdict "Accept as passed" and "Close as not verified", an approval "Approve", a stale check "Keep waiting" and "Accept without the host"; an act prompt none), each behind the same undo. Besides a brief's reply and a sign-in code (ADR-console-024), only two things are typed: a manual check's note in a textarea sent verbatim with "Send note", and the confirmation of every approval, which must equal `<owner>/<repo>#<issue>` before "Approve" enables (a paid one adds "This check costs money."). Number keys and Enter stay with a waiting brief and decision items and are ignored while an input, textarea, select or contenteditable element has focus; unsent text and focus survive every streamed frame. A decision prompt takes no typed reply (ADR-console-010), while a brief takes one. A brief is drawn as an open item: the goal above it, its version and its five parts, an empty list part shown as its label and "None" (#911), Enter starting the same five-second undo and then approving, and a reply field beneath that asks for changes, hinted "Or say what to change…" and named "Or say what to change" (ADR-console-013). An answered item is one closed quiet Overlay 2 line, never peach: its question cut to one line with an ellipsis, then "answered <age>, waiting for the agent"; the whole item (its meta line, the choices and the link) shows when the line is opened. Every tracker item carries the "Answer on the tracker" link (in its Detail fold, or as the primary action when the page cannot answer it), the page's only link besides a running sign-in's link (ADR-console-024); a brief has none, because the page answers it itself.
- **Digest.** Beneath the inbox, "Since you last looked" with the time, or "In the last eight hours" on a first visit: five plain lines, newest first, each in two columns, the age, then the sentence, the rest under a closed "Earlier" fold with its count; "Nothing changed." when empty. Quiet colours only: Overlay 2 times, Subtext 0 sentences. A session named for a sprint adds no line (#922).
- **Times.** The page formats every time against its own clock in local time and never shows a raw timestamp. An age reads relative: "just now", "N min ago", "N h ago", "yesterday", "N days ago"; a duration ("waiting 3 h", "untouched for 3 days") reads "under a minute", "N min", "N h", "1 day", "N days"; a reset or a resume reads a weekday and 24-hour time ("Mon 05:00"). A delivery run's "moved 3 min ago" or "quiet for 22 min" is the same: its words come from the run's movement time. Each is a `time` element whose title is the exact local time ("2026-11-11 09:00"). The console sends only changed views, so the page moves its times itself every 30 seconds without a frame, touching nothing but their text.
- **Last looked.** The page tells the console it was looked at when a focus hold of more than five seconds ends; it sends nothing else but an answer (ADR-console-010).
- **Palette (Catppuccin Macchiato roles).** Base `#24273a` page; Mantle `#1e2030` sunken fields; Surface 0 `#363a4f` the one raised card; Surface 1 `#494d64` and Surface 2 `#5b6078` borders; Text `#cad3f5`; Subtext 1 `#b8c0e0` the sentence; Subtext 0 `#a5adcb` secondary text; Overlay 2 `#939ab7` quiet lines and times; Crust `#181926` the text on a filled peach or Lavender control; Lavender `#b7bdf8` the focus ring, the owner's own words and the picked option; Peach `#f5a97f` means "needs you" and nothing else, in four uses: the statement's count, an open item's kind word, the card's one primary action and the state words and icon of a sprint waiting on the owner; Green `#a6da95`, Yellow `#eed49f` and Red `#ed8796` mark moving, waiting and stopped work and capacity's fine, tight and out; Red also marks "Could not send". Hairlines are `rgba(202, 211, 245, .09)`.
- **Type scale.** The system UI stack, never a web font. Body .9375rem/1.5 with tabular numerals; section headings (the digest's, Capacity and Work) one shared style, 600 .9375rem/1.4 in Text; the lead question 600 1.5rem/1.3 display, the largest type after the statement headline; option words 1.0625rem and key badges .71875rem; secondary text .84375rem; small text .8125rem; quiet lines .875rem; the statement headline 2rem under 480px.
- **Projects.** The side column is headed "Work", then "N sprints across M projects" in Overlay 2 (nothing after "Work" with none). A project is a block: its name a small 600 heading, a hairline between projects, its sessions summary and each delivery run's sentence as quiet lines beneath it, then its sprints (see Sprints). A quiet project is one line: its name, "quiet" with its icon, "untouched for N days", and at the right a "Give it a goal" button that opens the goal field with that project chosen, the filter cleared and the typed goal kept, focus in the goal. Its root, its sessions' names and states and each run's issue numbers and stages show only when the line is opened; with every line closed the page shows no path, session name or issue number. An unmanaged repository is the same heading, named by the last segment of its root. Three or fewer quiet projects stay lines; more than three fold into one closed group titled "N quiet projects". Projects with open work come first, then quiet ones, each by most recent activity. The view decides order and folding; the page only renders them.
- **Sprints.** A sprint is two lines. The first holds a state icon (pause waiting on you, clock waiting on quota, dashed circle quiet, play on track or starting, circle-check closing, tick done, lines drafting), the goal, and at the right one 9px pip per reported issue (landed filled Subtext 0, in delivery ringed Green, queued a Surface 2 outline, held two Yellow bars; never told apart by colour alone, never Peach) with "n of m", the pips wrapping within at most 40% of the row so a long report never widens it. The second holds the state in words in the icon's colour (Green moving, Yellow waiting on quota or quiet, Peach waiting on you, Overlay 2 drafting or done), the manager's status line and its age, "manager <what it is doing>" for its listed session, and the stated age. A sprint with no report shows no pips. The full progress sentence, the issues, the manager session's name and state, next, milestone and the tracker link show only in its "Detail" fold; the manager's start state and its retry, a withdrawal's failure, the newest message and "manager not reachable" stay under the two lines, the older messages sit in the "Detail" fold, and beneath them sit one quiet "Tell the manager" button and, at the right, a closed "More" fold holding "Withdraw" and, before it, "Stop" for a sprint that can be stopped; the question "Stop" opens, its "Confirm stop" and "Cancel", and a stop's failure stay on the sprint's line. "Tell the manager" opens its field and "Send" in place with the focus in the field; Escape in an empty field, or leaving an empty field for anything outside the sprint, closes it, a typed draft keeps it open, and a sent message closes it.
- **Motion and small screens.** Motion stays under 200ms and is off under `prefers-reduced-motion`; under 900px the card's side padding drops to 16px.
- **State is never colour alone.** Every state carries a word, and every icon sits beside one.
- **Empty fleet.** With no registered project the projects section says "No project is registered yet."

## Consequences

- The prototype worktree can be dropped once this page ships.
- A new colour role or a second use of peach is a change to this record.
