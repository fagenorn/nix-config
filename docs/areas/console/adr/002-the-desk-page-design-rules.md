# ADR-console-002 — The desk page's design rules

- **Status:** accepted; amended by ADR-console-024, ADR-console-025, ADR-console-028, ADR-console-029, ADR-console-031, ADR-console-032, ADR-console-033, ADR-console-035, ADR-console-038, ADR-console-052, ADR-console-056
- **Decided in:** #833, #855, #887, #895, #911, #919, #920, #922, #944, #1026, #1027, #1039, #1036, #1046, #1066, #1086, #1119, #1133, #1135, #1136, #1137, #1177, #1214

## Context

The owner chose the desk layout from a throwaway prototype that is never merged. Its rules are recorded here so later slices build the page without it.

## Decision

- **Structure.** A sticky top bar with the name, a quiet "Spend" link to the Spend page (ADR-console-052), the goal field and the capacity strip, with the accounts panel opening beneath it, then a desk grid: at most 1360px wide, a main column `minmax(0, 1fr)` and a 460px side column with an 88px gap (400px and 56px under 1180px; one column under 900px; under 480px the top bar wraps and the statement shrinks). The statement leads the main column; the projects sit in the side column.
- **Statement.** One large line says whether the owner is needed: "Nothing needs you", or the number of inbox items in peach followed by "thing needs you" or "things need you". When the tracker could not be read and no item is known, it reads "The tracker cannot be read"; it never says "Nothing needs you" then. One plain sentence sits beneath it, and beneath that a quieter line announces a manager's new reply ("New reply from the manager of <title>", or a count of them), which is not part of the count and never peach. Headline 650 weight, 3.5rem/1.04, display stack, -0.028em tracking (2.375rem, -0.022em under 900px); sentence 1.125rem/1.5 in Subtext 1, at most 44rem wide (1rem under 900px).
- **Goal field.** The goal field sits in the top bar: one line, "Tell a manager what you want next…", with its key `g` shown beside it. Its placeholder stays on one line, ending in an ellipsis when the field is narrower than it. Focusing it or pressing `g` puts the cursor in it and opens the project choice (the filter, the recent projects, Send and Cancel) in a panel beneath it, at most 600px wide and never wider than the viewport less 32px; Escape or Cancel closes the panel, keeps the typed text and leaves the field. Under the field the panel shows the goal's length against 2000, counted as the service counts it (code points after stripping, ADR-console-011): "<n> / 2000" in Overlay 2, "<n> / 2000, <m> left" in Text above 1800, and "<n> / 2000, <m> over the limit" in Red at weight 600 over 2000; a refusal line sits directly beneath it, above the project choice (#1214). In focus the field shows one border, its container's Lavender one; the text area draws no ring of its own. Nothing under the statement opens it. Under 900px the field takes its own row of the top bar.
- **Capacity strip.** The strip starts with one state word beside its icon, fine, tight or out, by the view's tight and all-out rules: fine is a tick in Green, tight a clock in Yellow, out a stop in Red, never Peach. The strip is one line and never shrinks: at 1440px all of it shows with nothing clipped, the goal field yielding width first down to its 12rem minimum; when even that leaves no room the strip moves to a second line of the top bar, and only a strip wider than the whole bar clips. After the state word it lists each account with a reading: its name, cut at 9rem with an ellipsis and whole in its title, a 34px bar filled to its share of the week left whenever that share is known, out or not, and one figure, "N%", or "out until <weekday time>" when out, or "reset, not yet used" when every window has reset, or "week unknown" when the week is not known; the 5-hour share sits in its title. An account whose next reset (its "out until" time when out) has passed by the page's clock reads "reset, not yet used" with no bar, in the strip and in the accounts panel, and the state word, the statement and the all-out list follow it, until a frame says more (#1045). The quiet Overlay 2 words "excluded" and "pinned" sit beside the name of an account that is; an excluded account's bar is dimmed to Surface 2 while its quiet word "excluded" stays; an account signed in under two profiles shows once. Sign-in words ("signed out", "signed in, ends in N days", "signed in, ends today") show only for a signed-out profile or one whose sign-in ends within three days, and a signed-in profile that is not asking carries none. A signed-out profile is one span, its name and "signed out", with no bar, percentage or out words, and its old reading never drives the state word. A profile with no reading is counted in the one quiet note, unless it is signed out, asking for a sign-in, excluded or pinned, which show by name; with no reading at all only the note shows. The strip carries no "resets <weekday time>" and no "resumes <weekday time>": a reset time is a detail of the accounts panel, and the statement keeps "Resumes" when every profile is out. With no account it reads "no accounts". The strip holds no button inside it, and the strip as a whole is the one control that opens the accounts panel. Under 480px it shows only the state word and the tightest account. The all-out panel names a signed-out profile "<name> signed out", never out.
- **Accounts panel.** The strip is the one control that opens the accounts panel, as does the `a` key (not while a field has the focus and not with a modifier key); Escape, Close or the strip again closes it, and the focus returns to where it was (to the page itself when nothing had it), else to the strip. While it is open only Escape acts among the page keys. It holds one row per account, naming the account its client reports and, when another profile is signed in to the same account, saying "same account as <name>", with its details (the week and 5-hour figures, the reset time, the end of its sign-in) and its controls: Exclude or Include (ADR-console-023), Pin or Unpin (ADR-console-023), Sign in or Sign in again with the run status of its sign-in (ADR-console-024), offered in every row whatever the sign-in's end and Remove with its typed-name confirmation (ADR-console-026). Beneath the rows sits the pin's not-in-effect sentence, and Add account sits at the bottom (ADR-console-025). A running sign-in's link and code field show in its row while the panel is open, else on its inbox card. The panel is drawn like the goal field's panel with the existing tokens and quiet buttons, no new colour role.
- **Inbox.** The inbox sits beneath the statement, ordered by held work. The first open item (the first waiting brief, else the first tracker item) is the one raised card: Surface 0, 16px radius, a head line with its kind in words and its icon (Decision, Brief, Check, Approval, Conflict, Label), its project and, at the right, how long it has waited. Its question is the heading (a brief's heading stays "<project> brief, version N"); a manual check's heading is the first sentence of its ask and its check id a small label after the kind words (beside the headline in a row, before it in an answered line), the rest of the ask staying in its detail (#1046), with "and N more questions" when there are more; a decision shows a question label above its options only when the item has more than one question; below the options one line says what it holds ("holds n issues", with "read" and the time of the last good read when its tracker read is stale), and the page shows no issue number. Each option is a full-width button: its number as a key badge, its words, and "Recommended" on the recommended one; the picked option carries `aria-pressed="true"`, a Lavender border and a filled badge, so it is never marked by colour alone. The card ends in exactly one primary action, peach with Crust text: "Send answer" for a decision, "Approve" for a brief, the first answer for a check or an approval, and for an item the page cannot answer the "Answer on the tracker" link itself. The primary names its key only where a key acts on it (Enter, on the decision or brief the keys act on); a check's or an approval's primary names none. Beside it sit the quieter actions: the other answers, the note, the reply field and "Ask for changes", and a closed "Detail" fold holding the "Answer on the tracker" link, where a conflict or needs-label item also names its issue and pull request and an item's list shows. The undo, sending and "Could not send" words sit where the primary was. Every other open item is one row, a waiting brief included, set below the card by the same gap as between rows: icon and kind word, headline (a brief's goal), project and held work (a brief's project alone), the wait at the right; a tracker item's answer box, a pending Undo, a failure and its closed folds stay beneath that line. A quiet key strip under the inbox lists only the keys that act now: the number range for the decision the keys act on, Enter for it once every question has a pick, Enter for a brief unless its reply is sending, Esc while an answer waits; it is absent otherwise. A sign-in ask is a card of its own, never the raised card and never a tracker row: the headline, the sign-in words and the Sign in action, and while a sign-in runs its link, a field for the code and Cancel (ADR-console-024), the card pointing to the accounts panel instead while the panel is open and shows that sign-in; sign-in cards sit after the stall and progress cards and before the questions and tracker items, in the order of the view's `inbox.open`. A waiting brief sits above every tracker item and, while it can be approved, takes the keys (ADR-console-013); otherwise the first answerable item (a decision with questions), wherever it sits in the list, is the one the keys act on, and a conflict or needs-label item leading the list never blocks it. A later waiting brief's row opens, by a click or Enter on it, to its whole unraised brief with its "Approve" and its reply field in place, and closing it keeps a typed reply; a row opens by itself once when its approval or reply gets under way, fails or is done, and the owner may close it again, and a row that receives the focused reply field when the items reorder opens so the field keeps its focus. When the card's item leaves, the next item becomes the card. The options of that item are the buttons above, the tracker's chosen one (else the recommended one) preselected; a number key selects an option of the current question and moves to the next, Enter starts a five-second undo ("Sending in five seconds." with an "Undo" button; Escape undoes too), then the answer is sent. An item with a pending send keeps its answer box and its Undo wherever a frame moves it, and the keys then act on the next answerable item. Each answer shows one of four states: choosing, waiting, "Could not send: ..." in Red with the choice kept, with a quiet Try again when a repeat may succeed (ADR-console-032), or "Answered, waiting for the agent to pick it up". An open question of an adoption plan (ADR-console-029 D10) is a question card: kind word "Question" and its title "Register <name>" in the head line with no project, its ask as the heading with its line breaks kept, one numbered option button per offered answer with nothing preselected, Enter with the same five-second undo, no words of the owner's own and no tracker link; a row of one keeps its option buttons and "Send answer" like the card, a question with no offered answer shows its why line and no button, a refusal shows on its card, and a sent answer reads "Answered, planning again" as the fifth state; a failed registration is the same kind of card with the options "Try again" and "Not now" and its words shown verbatim, and its sent answer reads "Answered, trying again" or "Answered, not now" (ADR-console-035). A held verification is an item of its own kind (ADR-console-012): its detail shows in full in a `pre` inside a closed fold ("Show the check", "Review and approve" for an approval, which also holds the confirmation line and field), and its buttons are by kind (a manual check "Passed" and "Failed", a verdict "Accept as passed" and "Close as not verified", an approval "Approve", a stale check "Keep waiting" and "Accept without the host"; an act prompt none), each behind the same undo. Besides a brief's reply, a sign-in code (ADR-console-024) and an account name (ADR-console-025), only two things are typed: a manual check's note in a textarea sent verbatim with "Send note", and the confirmation of every approval, which must equal `<owner>/<repo>#<issue>` before "Approve" enables (a paid one adds "This check costs money."). Number keys and Enter stay with a waiting brief and decision items and are ignored while an input, textarea, select or contenteditable element has focus; unsent text and focus survive every streamed frame. A decision prompt takes no typed reply (ADR-console-010), while a brief takes one. A brief is drawn as an open item: the goal above it, its version and its five parts, an empty list part shown as its label and "None" (#911), Enter starting the same five-second undo and then approving, and a reply field beneath that asks for changes, hinted "Or say what to change…" and named "Or say what to change" (ADR-console-013). An answered item is one closed quiet Overlay 2 line, never peach: its question cut to one line with an ellipsis, then "answered <age>, waiting for the agent"; the whole item (its meta line, the choices and the link) shows when the line is opened. Every tracker item carries the "Answer on the tracker" link (in its Detail fold, or as the primary action when the page cannot answer it), the page's only link besides a running sign-in's link (ADR-console-024); a brief has none, because the page answers it itself.
- **Digest.** Beneath the inbox, "Since you last looked" with the time, or "In the last eight hours" on a first visit: five plain lines, newest first, each in two columns, the age, then the sentence, the rest under a closed "Earlier" fold with its count; "Nothing changed." when empty. Quiet colours only: Overlay 2 times, Subtext 0 sentences. A session named for a sprint adds no line (#922).
- **Times.** The page formats every time against its own clock in local time and never shows a raw timestamp. An age reads relative: "just now", "N min ago", "N h ago", "yesterday", "N days ago"; a duration ("waiting 3 h", "untouched for 3 days") reads "under a minute", "N min", "N h", "1 day", "N days"; a reset or a resume reads a weekday and 24-hour time ("Mon 05:00"). A delivery run's "moved 3 min ago" or "quiet for 22 min" is the same: its words come from the run's movement time. Each is a `time` element whose title is the exact local time ("2026-11-11 09:00"). The console sends only changed views, so the page moves its times itself every 30 seconds without a frame, touching nothing but their text.
- **Last looked.** The page tells the console it was looked at when a focus hold of more than five seconds ends; it sends nothing else but an answer (ADR-console-010).
- **Palette (Catppuccin Macchiato roles).** Base `#24273a` page; Mantle `#1e2030` sunken fields; Surface 0 `#363a4f` the one raised card; Surface 1 `#494d64` and Surface 2 `#5b6078` borders; Text `#cad3f5`; Subtext 1 `#b8c0e0` the sentence; Subtext 0 `#a5adcb` secondary text; Overlay 2 `#939ab7` quiet lines and times; Crust `#181926` the text on a filled peach or Lavender control; Lavender `#b7bdf8` the focus ring, the owner's own words and the picked option; Peach `#f5a97f` means "needs you" and nothing else, in four uses: the statement's count, an open item's kind word, the card's one primary action and the ball words and icon of a sprint waiting on you; Green `#a6da95`, Yellow `#eed49f` and Red `#ed8796` mark moving, waiting and stopped work and capacity's fine, tight and out; Red also marks "Could not send", a danger button's words and a goal count over its limit; `--hover` is the existing hover tint of a control. Hairlines are `rgba(202, 211, 245, .09)`.
- **Type scale.** The system UI stack, never a web font. Body .9375rem/1.5 with tabular numerals; section headings (the digest's, Capacity and Work) one shared style, 600 .9375rem/1.4 in Text; the lead question 600 1.5rem/1.3 display, the largest type after the statement headline; option words 1.0625rem and key badges .71875rem; secondary text .84375rem; small text .8125rem; quiet lines and controls .875rem; the statement headline 2rem under 480px.
- **Controls.** One system styles every button and input on both pages, declared once in a marked controls block that the desk page and the Spend page carry identically; the desk adds only the primary, danger and option colours. There are four button kinds: `btn-primary` (a card's one peach action, "needs you", unchanged in meaning), `btn-quiet` (every other action, the choice chips that carry `aria-pressed`, and the two button-styled summaries "End the sprint" and "Detail"), `btn-danger` (Remove and Drop the sprint, their words in Red, never filled) and `btn-option` (the numbered answers). There are three input kinds: `field-line`, `field-area` and `field-code` (monospace, for sign-in codes). `btn-bare` and `field-bare` stand for a control whose content or container draws its frame. A later control, for example an Ask box, takes one of these kinds and adds no new look. Size: 32px minimum height (the primary 40px, an option 44px), 5px 12px padding (fields 5px 10px), 8px radius, .875rem/1.4 type (buttons weight 500, fields 400). States: hover is a tint and a Surface 2 border, the same on both pages and never on a disabled control (the primary keeps its lighter peach); keyboard focus is the 2px Lavender ring offset 2px on every kind; pressed is a 1px press, and `aria-pressed` gives a Lavender border and weight 600; disabled is the `disabled` or `aria-disabled` attribute with a dashed border, a not-allowed cursor and dimming; busy is `aria-busy` beside the disable, with a dotted border, a progress cursor and a trailing ellipsis (not on options); the account panel's controls stay `aria-disabled` while they post so they keep the focus. No state relies on colour alone. Transitions stay under 200ms and are off under reduced motion on both pages. Every control's hit target is at least 32px tall at 1440 and 480px, every input has a visible or accessible label, and a placeholder is Overlay 2 at full opacity, at least 4.5:1 against its field (#1137).
- **Projects.** The side column is headed "Work", then "N sprints across M projects" in Overlay 2 (nothing after "Work" with none). A project is a block: its name a small 600 heading, a hairline between projects, its sessions summary and each delivery run's sentence as quiet lines beneath it (each delivery run's line, when its project line is opened, ends in its spend figure, ADR-console-056) (its sprints sit above the projects, see Sprints); a project with nothing under it (not quiet and no session outside its sprints) is not drawn. A quiet project is one line: its name, "quiet" with its icon, "untouched for N days", and at the right a "Give it a goal" button that opens the goal field with that project chosen, the filter cleared and the typed goal kept, focus in the goal. Its root, its sessions' names and states and each run's issue numbers and stages show only when the line is opened; with every line closed the page shows no path, session name or issue number. An unmanaged repository is the same heading, named by the last segment of its root. Three or fewer quiet projects stay lines; more than three fold into one closed group titled "N quiet projects". Projects with open work come first, then quiet ones, each by most recent activity. The view decides order and folding; the page only renders them. Notices: a project with open notices has one closed, quiet "Notices (n)" fold in the project lines, right after its line, or in its place and named by the project when it has none; closed, it shows the same disclosure arrow as "Show the report" and "Finished sprints". Opened, each open notice is its own block, newest first, with a 12px gap between blocks: its first line is the kind in words and who posted it ("Claim, from <title>", "Heads-up, from <title>", "Hand-off for you, from <title>", "Hand-off for <title>, from <title>"), in Subtext 0, with its age at the end in Overlay 2; its second line is its words in Text; a claim's third line, in Overlay 2, is its paths in the code style and its "until <until>". A link or any other word of 24 or more characters stays on one line, cut with an ellipsis, its whole text in its title (#1133); a link is text, never a link colour. A hand-off for the owner stands out only by its words "Hand-off for you", never by a colour. The fold also shows for a project with a running sprint even with no notice, and holds a quiet "Post a notice" control that opens a one-line field with Send and Cancel, the draft and its focus surviving every frame and a refusal's reason beside it; the owner's own notice reads "Heads-up, from You" with a quiet Withdraw (#1120, ADR-console-053). A notice is information: never an inbox item, never peach, and it never counts in the statement (#1119). Ask: every registered project, quiet ones and those in a quiet group included, has one Ask block right after its line and Notices fold, or in their place named by the project: a quiet "Ask a question" button that opens in place with focus in it, a `field-area` "Question about <project>" with Send and Cancel; Escape in an empty field or Cancel closes it; the draft, its selection and its focus survive every frame; a refusal's reason sits beside Send with the draft kept; a sent question empties the field, leaves the box open and shows at once as working until the view carries it. Under it the project's questions, newest first: the question in Lavender with its age, then "Working on it", a timed-out or failed one's one plain line, or an answer: its question back first ("Question for you: ..."), the answer with its line breaks, then quiet "Read: ...", "Ran: ...", "Could not find out: ..." and the production line. A question is information: never an inbox item, never peach, never counted (#1177, ADR-console-059 D3). Register project: a quiet control at the foot of the side column, under the projects, opens a one-line path field with Register and Cancel; the typed path is a draft whose text, selection and focus survive every frame, a refused write shows its reason beside the control, and the latest result (its one line, then its change lines) shows beneath it with a Close that hides it on this page until another result arrives; while a registration card asks, the result is the one line `Register <name>: needs you`; a registered project's result is gone; an adoption under way shows its one line and the one pull request link, never a branch or a commit hash; a long path in an option or change line breaks after a `/` (ADR-console-035); the plan's open questions are inbox items, not part of that result (ADR-console-028, ADR-console-029 D10).
- **Sprints.** A running sprint is one block at the head of the Work column, above the project lines, bounded by a hairline: its project, its title, one line under its title, at the block's text width and at most two lines (its whole text in its title), of what it is doing now, built from its report's milestone and its issues' stages ("building 2 of 5: the upload form", "waiting for your answer: <the question>", "last seen <stage>" when nothing has moved), never the report's status line, and the ball in words with its icon (a play icon in Green and "manager working", a pause icon in Peach and "waiting on you", a dashed circle in Yellow and "nothing running, may be stuck"; never colour alone), and at the right one 9px pip per reported issue (landed filled Subtext 0, in delivery ringed Green, queued a Surface 2 outline, held two Yellow bars; never told apart by colour alone, never Peach) with "n of m", the pips wrapping within at most 40% of the row so a long report never widens it. A sprint with no report shows no pips. Its controls sit inside the block: the manager's start state and its retry, a drop's failure ("Could not drop") and "manager not reachable", one quiet "Tell the manager" button, and at the right a closed "End the sprint" fold holding "Drop the sprint" and, before it, "Stop and report" for a sprint that can be stopped; the question "Stop and report" opens, its "Stop and report" and "Cancel", and a stop's failure stay in the block. The block's age reads "goal set <age>". A running block carries, on its own line under its head, one quiet Overlay 2 spend figure linking to the sprint on the Spend page (ADR-console-056). The closed "Show the report" fold ("Show the goal" before the first report) holds the report's status line, then the report detail (the progress sentence, the issues, next, milestone and the tracker link) and the manager session's name and state. The block shows the sprint's latest exchange with the life of its message (sent, picked up, replied, or not picked up after 5 min with what the owner can do): the owner's words read "You: <words>" in Lavender beside the manager's "Manager: <reply>", and a closed "Earlier messages (n)" fold holds the older ones. A brief approval leaves the block once the manager has picked it up; a reply to it is the manager's own line, with no approval line above it. "Tell the manager" opens its box in place with the focus in the field: the label "Tell the manager", the field under it at the block's full width, the hint right under the field (when the manager picks the message up), then "Send"; Escape in an empty field, or leaving an empty field for anything outside the sprint, closes it, a typed draft keeps it open, and a sent message closes it. Running blocks come before the project lines. An ended sprint is no block: it is one line of the one closed "Finished sprints (n)" list, showing its title, its outcome and when it ended ("ended <age>"), the closing report (its summary, its counts, the landed, held and dropped issues, the lesson as "Next time: <text>" and the tracker link, in a report indented under its line with a left hairline) opening on demand from its line together with the sprint's last exchange; a finished line carries its total as the first line of its opened report (ADR-console-056). A new reply is marked "new" on its message row in a block, and "new reply" on the "Earlier messages (n)" fold that holds it, on a finished line and on the "Finished sprints (n)" summary.
- **Motion and small screens.** Motion stays under 200ms and is off under `prefers-reduced-motion`; under 900px the card's side padding drops to 16px.
- **State is never colour alone.** Every state carries a word, and every icon sits beside one.
- **Empty fleet.** With no registered project the projects section says "No project is registered yet." Register project shows there too (ADR-console-028).

## Consequences

- The prototype worktree can be dropped once this page ships.
- A new colour role or a second use of peach is a change to this record.
