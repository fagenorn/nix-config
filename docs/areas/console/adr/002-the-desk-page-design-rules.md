# ADR-console-002 — The desk page's design rules

- **Status:** accepted
- **Decided in:** #833, #855, #895

## Context

The owner chose the desk layout from a throwaway prototype that is never merged. Its rules are recorded here so later slices build the page without it.

## Decision

- **Structure.** A sticky top bar with the name, then a desk grid: at most 1360px wide, a main column `minmax(0, 1fr)` and a 460px side column with an 88px gap (400px and 56px under 1180px; one column under 900px). The statement leads the main column; the projects sit in the side column.
- **Statement.** One large line says whether the owner is needed: "Nothing needs you", or the number of inbox items in peach followed by "thing needs you" or "things need you". When the tracker could not be read and no item is known, it reads "The tracker cannot be read"; it never says "Nothing needs you" then. One plain sentence sits beneath it. Headline 650 weight, 3.5rem/1.04, display stack, -0.028em tracking (2.375rem, -0.022em under 900px); sentence 1.125rem/1.5 in Subtext 1, at most 44rem wide (1rem under 900px).
- **Inbox.** The inbox sits beneath the statement, ordered by held work. The first open item opens: its project and issue, its question, each decision question with its options as a list (the chosen one prefixed "chosen: ") or its items as a list, a meta line "waiting since" with "holds n issues", and, when its tracker read is stale, "read at" the time of the last good read; then the link. The other open items are one line each with the same facts. The first answerable item (a decision with questions), wherever it sits in the list, is the one the keys act on; a conflict or needs-label item leading the list never blocks it. Its options are buttons prefixed by their number, the tracker's chosen one (else the recommended one) preselected; a number key selects an option of the current question and moves to the next, Enter starts a five-second undo ("Sending in five seconds." with an "Undo" button; Escape undoes too), then the answer is sent. An item with a pending send keeps its answer box and its Undo wherever a frame moves it, and the keys then act on the next answerable item. Each answer shows one of four states: choosing, waiting, "Could not send: ..." in Red with the choice kept, or "Answered, waiting for the agent to pick it up". A held verification is an item of its own kind (ADR-console-012): its detail shows in full in a `pre`, and its buttons are by kind (a manual check "Passed" and "Failed", a verdict "Accept as passed" and "Close as not verified", an approval "Approve", a stale check "Keep waiting" and "Accept without the host"; an act prompt none), each behind the same undo. Only two things are typed: a manual check's note in a textarea sent verbatim with "Send note", and the confirmation of every approval, which must equal `<owner>/<repo>#<issue>` before "Approve" enables (a paid one adds "This check costs money."). Number keys and Enter stay with decision items and are ignored while an input, textarea, select or contenteditable element has focus; unsent text and focus survive every streamed frame. Answered items sit under "Answered, waiting for the agent to pick it up", one quiet Overlay 2 line each, never peach. Every item ends in the "Answer on the tracker" link, the page's only link.
- **Digest.** Beneath the inbox, "Since you last looked" with the time, or "In the last eight hours" on a first visit: five plain lines, newest first, each its age then its sentence, the rest under a closed "Earlier" fold with its count; "Nothing changed." when empty. Quiet colours only: Overlay 2 times, Subtext 0 sentences.
- **Times.** The page formats every time against its own clock in local time and never shows a raw timestamp. An age reads relative: "just now", "N min ago", "N h ago", "yesterday", "N days ago"; a duration ("waiting 3 h", "untouched for 3 days") reads "under a minute", "N min", "N h", "1 day", "N days"; a reset or a resume reads a weekday and 24-hour time ("Mon 05:00"). Each is a `time` element whose title is the exact local time ("2026-11-11 09:00"). The console sends only changed views, so the page moves its times itself every 30 seconds without a frame, touching nothing but their text.
- **Last looked.** The page tells the console it was looked at when a focus hold of more than five seconds ends; it sends nothing else but an answer (ADR-console-010).
- **Palette (Catppuccin Macchiato roles).** Base `#24273a` page; Mantle `#1e2030` sunken fields; Surface 0 `#363a4f` the one raised card; Surface 1 `#494d64` and Surface 2 `#5b6078` borders; Text `#cad3f5`; Subtext 1 `#b8c0e0` the sentence; Subtext 0 `#a5adcb` secondary text; Overlay 2 `#939ab7` quiet lines and times; Lavender `#b7bdf8` the focus ring and the owner's own words; Peach `#f5a97f` means "needs you" and nothing else; Green `#a6da95`, Yellow `#eed49f` and Red `#ed8796` mark moving, waiting and stopped work; Red also marks "Could not send". Hairlines are `rgba(202, 211, 245, .09)`.
- **Type scale.** The system UI stack, never a web font. Body .9375rem/1.5 with tabular numerals; section headings 600 .9375rem/1.4; secondary text .84375rem; small text .8125rem; quiet lines .875rem.
- **Quiet projects.** A quiet project is one line: its name in 600 Subtext 0, then its words in Overlay 2. Three or fewer stay lines; more than three fold into one closed group titled "N quiet projects". Projects with open work come first, then quiet ones, each by most recent activity. The view decides order and folding; the page only renders them.
- **State is never colour alone.** Every state carries a word, and every icon sits beside one.
- **Empty fleet.** With no registered project the projects section says "No project is registered yet."

## Consequences

- The prototype worktree can be dropped once this page ships.
- A new colour role or a second use of peach is a change to this record.
