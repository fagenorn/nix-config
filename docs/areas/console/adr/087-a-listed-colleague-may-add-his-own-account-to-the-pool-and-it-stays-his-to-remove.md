# ADR-console-087 — A listed colleague may add his own account to the pool, and it stays his to remove

Context: ADR-console-066 kept accounts owner-only and ADR-console-023 makes the console the chooser; the owner decided a listed colleague may add his own account (#1342 to #1345, #1361). Decision: his account serves his sprints first and anyone's when it has room, the owner's pin outranking that; Sign in waits for his consent to a sentence that the credential lives under the owner's user; he alone signs it in, he or the owner signs it out or removes it, and only the owner excludes it; the guest page labels it `yours` and the owner's page shows his login; a removal moves its managers at once to the pool's choice; spend stays the owner's. Rejected: only his own sprints on it, any sprint first on it, and no consent sentence.
