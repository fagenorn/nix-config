# ADR-console-038 — A manager's question shows its pictures on the card and full size in a viewer

Context: a manager's screenshot would settle a question, a brief or a report the owner reads on the page (#1090, #1091, #1092). Decision: a question, brief or report may carry up to eight of its sprint's own stored pictures (ADR-console-036), each with a line and optionally marked before or after. Its row's `pictures` list keeps them from the sweep. The card shows thumbnails (a report's in its Detail fold), and one shared modal viewer makes the rest of the page inert. The page fetches each picture once with its token into an object URL under `img-src data: blob:`. A question whose ask or context names screenshots or a `.png` without carrying a picture is refused (ADR-console-019). Rejected: an image `src` pointing at a console address or a host path, and a viewer for each card.
