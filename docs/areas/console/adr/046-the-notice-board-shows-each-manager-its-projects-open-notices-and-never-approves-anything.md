# ADR-console-046 — The notice board shows each manager its project's open notices and never approves anything

Context: a manager saw another manager's notices (ADR-console-044) only if it asked, and an overlap must reach the owner instead of a conflict (#1118). Decision: every manager is shown the open notices of the other sprints and of the owner (ADR-console-044) as one block derived on read: in its first prompt, after `sprint inbox` and after `sprint status`. A notice is never approval. The block ends with its claims' overlaps: the default rule that orders them is ADR-console-085's, and an overlap the rule cannot settle is one line on the owner's page, not a card, also ADR-console-085's. Rejected: storing the block, and the console refusing work over a claim.
