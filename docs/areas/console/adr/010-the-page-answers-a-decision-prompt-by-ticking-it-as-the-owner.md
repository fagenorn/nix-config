# ADR-console-010 — The page answers a decision prompt by ticking its box as the owner

Context: half of the owner's trips to the tracker were a tick of the recommendation, and the tracker must stay the record of the answer (#862). Decision: the page answers a tracker-born decision prompt by ticking exactly the chosen box as the owner, through the owner's own gh sign-in, after a five-second undo, so a delivery reads the tick as before. Rejected: the console keeping its own copy of the answer, because the console would become a second record; and a free-text reply, because the prompt's boxes are the answer.
