# ADR-console-050 — A sprint waiting on CI, a committing delivery or a release step reads what it waits for

Context: a sprint waiting on CI, a commit or a release read "may be stuck" (#1110). Decision: a wait is a movement signal at its last-seen time: CI, a committing delivery, a release step and, fourth, the land lock wait, which with its own forge call is ADR-console-061's. They are read every two minutes for each project with a running sprint, from the forge (a pulls query and the lock read) and the checkout, and kept in memory. The now-sentence names a wait only when the waits alone move the sprint, in that order, after a shown merge refusal (ADR-console-048). A check or release held on a person counts for nothing. Rejected: counting a `WAITING` check or an `attention_required` release, which would hide a sprint stuck on a person.
