# ADR-console-044 — A manager posts, lists and withdraws short notices for its project

Context: the managers of one project, each with a sprint of its own, had no way to tell each other what they claim, so the owner relayed it by hand (#1117). Decision: a manager posts, lists and withdraws bounded notices (claim, heads-up and hand-off, at most five open per sprint) on three manager writes. Notices are kept kinds, a one-way schema step, kept only in the console's log. A manager's notice ends when it is withdrawn or its sprint ends, and never grants or denies anything; what a claim names and its end when its slice lands are ADR-console-085's, and the owner's own notices ADR-console-053's. Rejected: the tracker or a commit as their home, and a claim that blocks work like a lock.
