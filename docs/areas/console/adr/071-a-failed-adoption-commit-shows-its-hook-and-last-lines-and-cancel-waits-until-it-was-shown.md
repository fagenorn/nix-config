# ADR-console-071 — A failed adoption commit shows its hook and last lines, and Cancel waits until it was shown

Context: an adoption commit failed inside a pre-commit hook, the card gave no reason, and Cancel removed the kept copy before the owner could learn why (#1236). Decision: the adoption tool keeps the failing hook, read from git's trace2 events, and the output's last lines; the card names the hook and shows the last 8 sanitized lines under the page pattern; Cancel is refused while that card has not been sent to a page stream. Rejected: running the hook again to find it, and entering the development environment or retrying unsigned, which #1249 owns.
