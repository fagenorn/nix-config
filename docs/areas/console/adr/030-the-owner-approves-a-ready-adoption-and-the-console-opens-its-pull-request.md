# ADR-console-030 — The owner approves a ready adoption on the page and the console opens its pull request

Context: applying a ready plan needed a terminal (#1054, #1073). Decision: `Apply it` on the card has the console apply the plan in its own worktree and push exactly the applied commit to one `adopt-<12 hex>` branch. It opens one pull request (looked up before and after a failed open), and a failed step resumes from its last row. The owner merges that pull request from its card, pinned to the recorded head, and the forge's required checks and its refusal of a moved head keep merge safety; the card, its merge and a failing check are ADR-console-088's. Rejected: the console merging without the owner, and pushing the local branch rather than the recorded commit.
