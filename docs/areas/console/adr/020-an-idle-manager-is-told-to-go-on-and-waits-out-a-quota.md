# ADR-console-020 — A manager idle while its sprint has work is told to go on, and waits out a quota

Context: a listed manager that went idle while its sprint had work stopped the sprint until the owner noticed. Decision: an idle manager is nudged with one fixed sentence, at most three times without a report. A running delivery or a stated wait holds the nudge back. Then one inbox item hands the sprint to the owner. Nothing is sent while the account is out, but a listed idle manager whose account is out is stopped and taken over on another account with room, counting as a bring-back (ADR-console-017), and waits as before when none has room (#1378). Rejected: leaving an idle listed manager alone, unbounded nudging, and sending to an account that is out.
