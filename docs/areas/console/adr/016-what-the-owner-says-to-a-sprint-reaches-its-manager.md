# ADR-console-016 — What the owner says to a sprint reaches its manager

Context: the owner must be able to say anything to a sprint, and #831 found how to reach a running background session or wake a stopped one. Decision: every message is stored first and read by the manager with `sprint inbox` under the sprint's secret. The console only nudges, with one fixed sentence: a relay to a listed session, else a wake in place of its session id. A delivered message the manager has not read five minutes after the relay that reached it is relayed once more, and still unread five minutes later the manager's listed idle session is stopped and woken in place; a manager working or waiting at a prompt is never stopped (#1379). Rejected: carrying the owner's words in the nudge, because they travel only through the authenticated inbox; and `--resume --bg` on a listed session, which starts a copy.
