# ADR-console-093 — The owner deletes a question thread, and the Ask box refuses a credential

Context: an owner who pasted a secret into the Ask box could not take it back, since the log keeps a question 90 days, a follow-up carries its thread, and only retention deletes (ADR-console-001, #1272). Decision: a credential-shaped question (`project_question.CREDENTIAL_SHAPES`) is refused first and nothing of it is kept; the owner deletes a whole thread with `POST /api/project-question-delete`, an owner-only page write that removes its rows with `secure_delete` and writes one kept `project_question.deleted` record of ids only, the one delete besides retention; a thread with a running session is refused. Rejected: relying on the shape detector alone, which misses unknown shapes, and leaving a pasted secret until retention removes it.
