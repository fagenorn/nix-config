# ADR-console-011 — A goal stated on the page becomes a sprint

Context: work starts when the owner says what they want (#834). Decision: a goal stated on the page is a sprint, recorded only in the console's own log and kept indefinitely. Each sprint gets a private secret, which later proves its manager. Rejected: putting the goal on the tracker, because a goal is the owner's sentence and not a piece of work, and nothing else may read it as one.
