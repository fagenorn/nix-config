# ADR-console-092 — A consequential card opens with nothing picked

Context: a fresh look card opened with "Approve this head" already picked, so one stray Enter could give an approval the owner never chose (#1265). Decision: a round question may carry `consequential: true`, every look card is consequential without the mark, and a consequential card opens with no option picked, so Send answer waits until the owner picks an option or types words, the Recommended badge staying; the tracker decision prompts of ADR-console-010 keep their preselection. Rejected: preselecting the recommended option on every card, and tying the unpicked opening to `owner_only`, since who may answer and whether the card opens unpicked are separate questions.
