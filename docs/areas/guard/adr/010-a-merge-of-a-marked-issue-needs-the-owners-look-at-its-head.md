# ADR-guard-010 — A merge of a marked issue needs the owner's look at its head

Context: an issue could hold a merge for the owner's look only in prose, and four slices of one issue merged unseen; the merge is the step the guard adjudicates. Decision: a look marker line in an issue's body (markers decide, labels filter: ADR-claims-001) makes the guard refuse a merge of a pull request that settles the issue unless a trusted (`OWNER` or `MEMBER`, ADR-verification-004) look approval names the issue, the pull request and its head or an earlier head the current head keeps, because no later commit changed the look files declared at the base commit or a live carry line attests it; any unreadable read refuses, and the merge must carry `--match-head-commit`. Rejected: a label or a prose hold, which binds nothing, and one approval per head, which would demand a new look after every sync merge.
